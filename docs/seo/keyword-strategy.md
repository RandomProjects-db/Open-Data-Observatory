## How it is used

1. Each keyword maps to one page, so pages do not compete for the same query.
2. Titles and summaries of mapped pages include the keyword in natural wording.
3. After 4 to 6 weeks of Search Console data, replace guesses with queries that actually bring impressions, and drop keywords with none.
